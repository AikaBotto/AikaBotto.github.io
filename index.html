How big is it, really?

Everything in the sky is measured in degrees across, not miles. Pick two things and see them at true relative size. The Moon is the ruler: about half a degree.

Angular size = how wide something looks from here, in degrees. 1° = 60 arcminutes = 3600 arcseconds. Values are typical / mean; the Moon and Sun wobble a few percent over the year, and planets change a lot with distance. Built by @AikaBotto.
