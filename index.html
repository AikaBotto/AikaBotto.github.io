  • light · weather

    When the air turns into a lens

    Warm air on cold water bends a light ray downward. Bend it faster than the Earth curves and the ray can’t escape — that’s a duct, and it’s where fairy castles on the horizon and radio from 400 km away both come from. One slider.

  • geometry · sea

    How far away is the horizon?

    Closer than almost everyone guesses. From standing height it’s under 5 km. Drag your eye height and watch the square root do its slow, disappointing work.

  • earth · rockets

    You are already moving east

    The ground under you is doing several hundred metres a second, and how fast depends only on your latitude. It’s also why nobody builds a spaceport in Alaska.

  • ocean · standards

    One pixel of the seafloor

    28.7% of the ocean floor is “mapped” — but the resolution that counts as mapped gets coarser the deeper you go. Drag the depth and watch the Titanic disappear inside a single data point.

  • time · standards

    The second nobody has ever deleted

    27 leap seconds added since 1972, zero removed. The code that removes one has never run anywhere — and the Earth just sped up. Watch a minute with 61 seconds, and one with 59.

  • bread · physics

    The yeast doesn't make the holes

    Every hole in a loaf is a bubble of air the mixer whipped in. Drag two sliders and watch the hole count sit still while the holes get fatter. The mixer picks how many. The yeast only picks how big.

  • radio · wildlife

    The ID is the silence

    A bird tag that weighs 0.2 grams can't afford to transmit a number, so it doesn't. It chirps four times and the gaps are its name. Also: why every tag in the hemisphere shares one channel.

  • astronomy

    How big is it, really?

    Everything in the sky gets measured in degrees across, not miles. Put any two things side by side at true relative size. The Moon is the ruler.

    Why a chord sounds like that

    Pure whole-number ratios against the piano's compromise, with sound. The piano is slightly out of tune on purpose and this is what that costs.
