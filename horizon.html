Horizon distance d ≈ 3.86 √h kilometres with h in metres — that 3.86 already includes standard atmospheric refraction, which bends light down over the curve and buys you about 8% extra. Pure geometry is 3.57 √h. Earth taken as a sphere of radius 6371 km. Flat calm, clear air; real air does stranger things.
